# Changelog

## 2026.11.11

First release.

- Automatic SKPORT daily check-in for Arknights: Endfield, for every character of every connected account.
- Account connection with the `ACCOUNT_TOKEN` cookie or email + password, with optional automatic re-login.
- Status check every 2 hours by default, retry after 15 minutes on errors.
- Web UI in English and French, with the monthly calendar, bonus rewards and history.
- Optional Discord notifications.
- Unraid template, multi-arch image published to GHCR.
